The 6 Pillars of AI Strategy

Designing, implementing, and governing intelligent systems on Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, and in agnostic cloud environments. These are the six disciplines that define modern AI architecture.

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Why it matters: RAG allows AI models to answer questions using real-time, domain-specific data without requiring full model retraining. It connects language models to your knowledge bases, documents, and databases — making AI responses accurate, current, and grounded in facts.

Common Pitfall: Poor document chunking strategies, weak embedding models, and lack of relevance scoring lead to irrelevant context being injected into prompts — causing confident but incorrect responses (hallucinations). RAG without strong design is worse than no RAG at all.
Vertex AI SearchAWS Bedrock KBOpenSearch
MCP

Model Context Protocol

Why it matters: MCP standardizes how AI models connect to external tools, APIs, databases, and services. It provides a structured contract so that agents can call tools safely and predictably — enabling AI systems to take real actions in the world beyond just generating text.

Common Pitfall: Exposing sensitive tools without authentication or input validation transforms your AI agent into an attack surface. Unvalidated tool calls can trigger destructive actions in production systems. MCP requires a security-first design from day one.
Anthropic MCPLangChain ToolsCustom APIs
A2A

Agent-to-Agent Communication

Why it matters: A2A enables autonomous agents to collaborate, delegate tasks, and share context with each other. Rather than a single monolithic AI trying to solve everything, specialized agents work together — creating systems that are more capable, modular, and maintainable.

Common Pitfall: Undefined agent boundaries and missing fallback logic lead to circular delegation loops, conflicting outputs, and infinite recursion. Without clear ownership contracts between agents, the system becomes impossible to debug and prone to silent failures.
CrewAIAutoGenLangGraph
Orchestration

Orchestration Agents

Why it matters: Orchestration agents coordinate complex, multi-step workflows across systems, models, tools, and services. They maintain state, manage parallel execution, handle retries, and ensure that complex AI pipelines reach their goals reliably — even in the presence of failures.

Common Pitfall: Building orchestrators without observability or state management creates brittle, black-box pipelines. When something fails mid-workflow with no tracing or replay capability, recovering the state becomes impossible. Tight coupling between workflow steps makes even small changes break everything.
Vertex AI PipelinesStep FunctionsTemporal
MLOps

MLOps — Machine Learning Operations

Why it matters: MLOps automates the full ML model lifecycle: data versioning, training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and automatic retraining. It brings DevOps discipline to AI — ensuring that models perform reliably in production and continuously improve as data evolves.

Common Pitfall: Skipping data versioning, ignoring model drift, and performing manual deployments are the top reasons production models silently degrade. A model that was accurate at launch can become dangerously wrong six months later if no monitoring or automated retraining pipeline exists.
Vertex AI MLOpsSageMakerMLflowKubeflow
SecGov

Security & AI Governance

Why it matters: AI systems that operate without security guardrails and governance frameworks introduce legal, ethical, and operational risk. Governance defines who controls AI decisions, how outputs are audited, what data may be used, and how the system complies with regulations such as GDPR, the EU AI Act, and sector-specific mandates in banking and healthcare.

Common Pitfall: Deploying AI without explainability, audit trails, or access controls creates black-box systems that cannot be inspected or regulated. Prompt injection attacks, data leakage through model outputs, biased decision-making, and uncontrolled agent actions are all consequences of treating governance as an afterthought rather than a design requirement.
OWASP AIEU AI ActVertex AI SafetyAWS Guardrails
